### Alexander the Great
No surviving autobiography by Alexander exists. Major literary sources were written centuries after his death and must be compared rather than treated as eyewitness transcripts.

Study:
- Arrian, *Anabasis of Alexander*: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/46976
- Plutarch, *Life of Alexander*: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Plutarch/Lives/Alexander*/home.html
- Diodorus Siculus, Book XVII: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Diodorus_Siculus/17A*.html

Research question for every episode: which source reports this, how late is it, what agenda/genre shapes it, and what corroborates it?

### Julius Caesar

## Hermeticism

Ancient Hermetic material must be separated from later occult/New Age claims.

- *Corpus Hermeticum*: prefer scholarly translations in print/library editions; public web editions vary in age and scholarship.
- Historical reference starting point: Stanford Encyclopedia and academic histories of late antiquity/Renaissance magic.
- *The Kybalion* is a modern 1908 work, not an ancient Egyptian/Hellenistic Hermetic scripture.
- Epistemic label for supernatural/metaphysical claims: ESOTERIC/METAPHYSICAL unless independently scientifically supported.

## Reading protocol

For important historical/philosophical topics:
1. Context
2. Primary text
3. Modern scholarly explanation
4. Competing interpretation
5. Criticism/source limitations
6. Personal synthesis

Never convert "old" into "true," "primary" into "unbiased," or "famous" into "reliable."
