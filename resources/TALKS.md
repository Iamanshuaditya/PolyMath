## How to curate the eventual Top 100

An item enters the Top 100 only if it scores highly on:
1. originality,
2. expertise,
3. information density,
4. durability,
5. cross-disciplinary value,
6. lack of duplication,
7. availability of an original/full source.

A 5-minute viral edit of a 2-hour conversation is normally excluded when the complete conversation is publicly available.

## Listening protocol

For long-form talks/interviews, capture only:
- central thesis,
- strongest argument/evidence,
- useful models,
- claims needing verification,
- one disagreement,
- one application.

Do not transcribe entertainment into a knowledge base merely because it sounds insightful.
